# 🎪 Lewi Palance — Organiser Edition aka Palance Organiser

## Vision
The operating system for Caribbean event organizers — create, promote, sell, check in, and get paid. Built for the realities of island logistics, local payments.  

---

## 🔐 Organiser Login & Access
Organizers use the same login account user as customers, with an **Organiser ACESS Mode** with extra profile setup.

 

Organizers can hold **both** a customer account and an organizer account under one login.

---

## 🏢 Organization Profile

Every organizer is an **Organization** — even solo promoters.

**Profile includes:**
- user profile
- Logo, cover, bio, country/city
- Verified badge tier
- Contact channels  
- Payout currency & method
- Public listing page (all Owned + Shared/Collab listings)
- Follower count (customers can follow the org)
- Rating aggregate (from reviews)



---

## 📦 Listing Management

**Create listings across categories:**
- 🎉 Events (concerts, fetes, conferences, parties)
- 🏨 Accommodation
- 🌴 Vacation experiences
- 🛠️ Services
- 🛍️ Store items 
- these listings do not have a cart just catalog items

**Per listing:**
- Title, description, media (image/video/reel-ready)
- Date/time, recurrence rules (for weekly parties, monthly markets)
- Location + map pin
- Ticket tiers (Early Bird, General, VIP, Table, Comp)
- Capacity & inventory
- Visibility: curstomer/organiser listing  (Public / Followers-only / Unlisted (link-only) )
-  permissions restrictions/allowed,  
- Collaborators attached
- Price (multi-currency),


**Draft → Review → Publish → Live → Archived** lifecycle. Publishing requires term subscriptions 1 month/3months/6months/1year

**Sub-accounts / Team:**
- Owner, Manager, Check-In Staff, Finance — role-based permissions
- Check-In Staff can only scan, not issue refunds
---

## 🔗 Collaborator Management

- Invite collaborators by org handle
- Set role: Co-host / Co-manager / Featured partner
- Collaborator appears on the listing + the listing appears on their profile
 - Collaborator approval flow (they must accept before listing goes live)

## 🎟️ Ticketing & Sales

**Ticket types:**
- Free RSVP (with optional approval)
- Paid (single or multi-tier for selected listings)
 
- **Fee** — customer pays 2%  
- **Fee** — organizer pays 2% + US$0.50
**Fee handling — organizer chooses:**
- **Absorb** — organizer pays customers 2% plus there 2% + US$0.50 
- **Hybrid** — absorb on early bird, pass on at the door or later tickets/by catolog items

**Payment rails per country:**
- Local: WiPay, Paywise, bank transfer, cash on delivery (via agent)
- International: Stripe, PayPal
- Payout schedule: configurable (weekly, post-event, on-demand)

**Sales dashboard:** based on subscriptions
- Live ticket count, revenue, check-ins
- Breakdown by tier, channel, country
- Refund & transfer management
- Promo codes and comp tickets

---

## ✅ Check-In (Organizer Console)

**Multi-mode scanning:**
- 📱 QR scanner (phone camera)
- 📶 NFC tap (Android + iOS) 
- ⌨️ Manual code entry (fallback)

**Check-In Staff console:**
- Works offline — syncs when signal returns
- Shows attendee name, tier, photo (if uploaded), special notes
- Flags: duplicate scan, wrong event, unpaid, VIP
- Real-time headcount
- Multiple scanners per event, synced

**Door tools:**
- Sell tickets at the door (cash + card)
 
- Issue refunds (Manager+ only)

## 💰 Revenue & Payouts

**Organizer pays:**
- 2% + US$0.50 per paid ticket (configurable per country)
- Optional: listing boost, sponsored reel, 

**Organizer receives:**
- Payout in local currency or USD
- Statement per event, per payout cycle
- Tax documents per country

## 🚀 Organizer Onboarding Flow

1. Sign up (email + phone)
2. Create Organization profile
3. Verify (business reg or ID)
4. Add payout method
5. Create first listing (guided template)
6. Publish or schedule
7. Share to WhatsApp
8. Sell, check in, get paid

**Target: first listing live in under 10 minutes.**
